Kitchen Rat

Hungry pups are waiting in the nest behind the wall. Bring them 20 food before dawn. Every theft raises your wanted level: first the cat gets wary, then come mousetraps, the owner with a flashlight, and poison.

WASD move · mouse look · Space jump · E sniff · F knock over · Shift run · Esc pause · F11 fullscreen

WASD move, mouse camera (click the game first), Space jump when the marker shows, E or right mouse button sniff (reveals cheese, poison and nearby traps), F knock things over, Shift run (noisy), Esc pause. Wanted stars rise from thefts, noise and being seen. Temporary stars fade when you hide and stay out of sight, faster in the nest. Neither the cat nor the owner can see you under furniture.
